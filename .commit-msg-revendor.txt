## Contract Changes (bd70412f)

### policy_digest
- Must be exactly 64 LOWERCASE hex chars (pattern ^[a-f0-9]{64}$)
- No "sha256:" prefix allowed in envelope
- Uppercase or malformed → REJECT_UNKNOWN
- Well-formed but different → DIGEST_MISMATCH

### execution_constraints VALUE validation
- budget_usd, max_request_usd: non-negative numbers (not booleans)
- max_latency_ms: non-negative integer (1.0 ok, 1.5 not; not booleans)
- risk_ceiling: enum {unknown, low, medium, high, critical}
- allowed_models, allowed_tools, allowed_agents, required_verification:
  arrays of non-empty strings
- expires_at: string
- Any violation → REJECT_UNKNOWN
